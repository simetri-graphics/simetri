This was my second time (in 2025 I presented [a paper](https://archive.bridgesmathart.org/2025/bridges2025-29.html#gsc.tab=0) at Bridges Eindhoven 2025) at a Bridges Math Art Conference. I presented the [Symmetries of Prime Factors paper](https://archive.bridgesmathart.org/2026/bridges2026-109.html#gsc.tab=0) on Friday, 7 August at 5 pm. I had to make significant changes to my presentation due to the late-Friday-afternoon time slot (considering that everybody is already tired and hungry). I came across this topic by accident. I know almost nothing about prime numbers (I knew even less before I wrote the paper). Compared to the other attendees of the conference, my mathematical background is very limited and involves only applied math (engineering applications, physics simulations, computer graphics) and no pure math or number theory. During the presentation I did not talk much about prime numbers, my focus was on transformations that generated the patterns. I finished it with animations of physical simulation of reflections (both affine and conformal). It was a very fast-paced (when I am tired I cannot follow slow-paced presentations), visually heavy presentation.

[Martin Skrodzki](https://ms-math-computer.science/) was the chair of the regular papers and he also edited the [conference proceedings](https://www.lulu.com/shop/martin-skrodzki-and-kerry-mitchell-and-katherine-seaton-and-eve-torrence/bridges-galway-2026-conference-proceedings/paperback/product-957ze9e.html?q=Bridges+conference+proceedings&page=1&pageSize=4) along with [Kerry Mitchell](https://kerry-mitchell.pixels.com/),
[Katherine Seaton](https://scholars.latrobe.edu.au/kaseaton/publications), and [Eve Torrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eve_Torrence). This is an incredible amount of work, and I would like to congratulate them for the great book they have designed and produced. And, I was pleasantly surprised that they used some images from my conference paper on the spine of the proceedings book.


[Craig S. Kaplan](https://isohedral.ca/) was the chair of the session that included my presentation. If you have **any** interest in computer graphics, you have most likely seen his work. Throughout the years I have learned a lot from his incredibly high quality work. He has become the new President of the Board of Directors for the Bridges Organization. Congratulations to him for the well-deserved position. 

On Thursday afternoon, 6 August, I met [Kerry Mitchell](https://kerry-mitchell.pixels.com/), and he generously gave me one of his books. [His artwork](https://www.kerrymitchell.art/) is incredible. It makes me wish that I had one tenth of his artistic talent. You can purchase his books at [lulu.com](https://www.lulu.com/search?contributor=Kerry+Mitchell&page=1&pageSize=10&adult_audience_rating=00&sortBy=PRODUCT_SALES_90_DAYS). He told me that he used [Ultra Fractal](ultrafractal.com) for creating some (most?) of his work. A long time ago I briefly used Ultra Fractal for generating fractal images but I did not know that it could be used to the extent he was able to take it. I need to check this out.

My wife, Aylin Sertkaya, was with me during my visit to Ireland. My presentation date was our 35th wedding anniversary as well. The final slide of the presentation was dedicated to her. I think it caught her off guard!
